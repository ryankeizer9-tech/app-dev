# My Favorite Series: The Walking Dead

**The Walking Dead** is a post-apocalyptic horror drama series based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman. It follows a group of survivors trying to stay alive in a world overrun by zombies, known as *walkers*.

## Main Characters
* **Rick Grimes** – A former sheriff's deputy who becomes the group's leader.
* **Daryl Dixon** – A skilled tracker and survivalist who uses a crossbow.
* **Michonne** – A fierce survivor known for using a katana.
* **Carol Peletier** – A survivor who grows into one of the group's toughest members.

## Top 3 Seasons
1. Season 1 (The outbreak and the journey to Atlanta)
2. Season 5 (The group reaches Alexandria)
3. Season 4 (The fall of the prison)

## Notable Quote
> "We are the walking dead." — *Rick Grimes*
